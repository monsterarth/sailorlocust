A tamed locust in a sailor's hat that takes the helm of a boat and sails it between named docks. Lines that run by themselves, ports with several berths, boats that queue and take turns, and boats that go on sailing while nobody is near.

What you get

  • Sailor locust (item): use it on a boat and it stands by the helm. Right click the helm seat (or press O on board, or in the water near the boat) for its sailing orders: click the docks and buoys it knows to add stops, move them up and down, set how long it waits at each dock. After the last stop it starts over. Sneak and right click to take it back.
  • Dock post (block): a named stop. It looks the way you face when you place it: the boat lies in front of it, side to the pier. A yellow outline on the water shows the berth and its lanes while you hold it.
  • Route buoy (block): a named point the boat passes without stopping, to lead it out of a harbour, through a channel or up a river.
  • Route wand (creative only): shows the way a locust has planned.

Ports

  • Docks with the same name are the berths of one port. A boat asks for a berth as it nears the port, and waits off the port when none is free: first come, first served.
  • A berth is only given to a boat that fits along the pier and has a clear way in, free of blocks and of boats lying there. A dock can be kept for small boats only.
  • Coming in or leaving, a boat keeps the water it works in and the others wait their turn. Long boats come in along the pier and are tied alongside; with no room to turn, they leave astern.
  • Port beacons (optional): name a buoy after the port and give it a role in its sheet: waiting area (blue), way out (green), way in (yellow, passed by every boat bound for the port).

Out of sight

A boat under orders with no player near leaves the world and sails on by time along its route, at the speed it really sailed; it comes back on the water, with its locust at the helm, when someone comes near. A boat with a player seated in it never leaves the world.

How it finds its way

  • The long route comes from world generation's ocean map, so it knows the way around land without the chunks being generated. It does not see lakes, rivers or islets: buoys are for those.
  • Around the boat it looks at the real blocks and the other boats, and steers clear of shores, piers and hulls.
  • The locust sets the boat's own speed and turning as a player's keys would, and the sail on boats that have one: each boat keeps its own handling. Works on the game's boats and on Shipwright's.

For server staff

ModConfig/sailorlocust.json: EnableRecipes, OceanThreshold, ArrivalRadius, BuoyPassRadius, BerthAskDistance, DefaultWaitSeconds, CruiseSail, OwnerOnly (only who placed a locust may change its orders), OffscreenSailing, HideUnseen, UnseenDistance, Debug.
Commands (privilege controlserver): /sailor list, /sailor fleet, /sailor route <from> <to>. Staff also see every boat under orders on the world map (layer "Sailor Locust: boats").
